# Liquids

## Greek Marinade

Other
* 1/2 peeled and julienned red onion
* 3 mashed garlic cloves, or minced equivalent

Wet Ingredients

* Juice of 1 lemon
* `1/4 cup` red wine vinegar (sub: apple cider vinegar)
* `1/2 cup` of Extra Virgin Olive Oil

Dry Ingredients
* `1 1/2 tsp` dry oregano
* `1 tsp` rosemary or thyme
* `1 tsp` paprika
* `1 tsp` cumin
* `1 tbsp` sea salt (or leave it out, I do)
* `1/2 tsp` black pepper

Mix it all together well in a bowl and add whatever it is you're going to marinate.

## Maple soy glaze

* `1/4 cup` soy / aminos
* `3 tbsp` rice venegar (unseasoned)
* `3 tbsp` maple syrup

## Sorta famous potato soup

* 1 empty 5 gallon bucket previously used for hard boiled egges
* gallon of milk
* a bunch of pepper
* 3 qts heavy cream
* 1 big can of potatoes
* 3 big cans of cream of potato soup, campbells specifically

mix it all up. good soup.


# Mains

## Chicken

### Greek Marinade Kebobs

Cut up chicken breast into 1 inch chunks, add to [Greek Marinade]({{< ref "recipes.md#greek-marinade">}}) from above and let it marinate for 4 hours minimum, 24 hours maximum. Put on skewers with whatever else you feel like and grill at high heat until cooked through.

Alternatively, skip cutting it up and just marinate whole chicken breast and grill that. Whatever you feel like.
